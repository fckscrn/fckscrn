Analytics (only with your consent)

On the public pages (home, help, create an event) we use Google Analytics 4 via Google Tag Manager to see what people look for and where they get stuck. It starts only after you click “Allow”. Without consent no analytics cookies are stored (Google Consent Mode).

CookieByPurposeLifetime
_gaGoogletells visitors apart (anonymous ID)2 years
_ga_*Googlesession state2 years

Data is processed by Google Ireland Ltd. and may be transferred to the USA (EU–US Data Privacy Framework). We measure anonymously, no names or e-mails.

Never measured: event admin, speaker links, room computers, the speaker's phone, audience pages (questions and polls) and door signs. Once an event is created, measuring stops on that page so the secret link never leaves it.
