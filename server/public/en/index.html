Why it exists

I've spoken at many conferences. Every time they promised everything was ready and that they do their best for speakers. Then I walked on stage and heard that sentence. The slides didn't load, presenter view ended up on the projector, the notes were nowhere.

It's not about technology. Nobody owns the whole thing from start to finish. FckScrn is my answer to that sentence.

Tomáš Foltas

How it works

  1. Create an eventOne room or several. Put the programme together in minutes, start times fill in by themselves.
  2. Speakers upload their slidesEach gets their own link. They upload PowerPoint, Keynote or PDF and immediately see how it will look. We check the format, videos and notes.
  3. One click in the roomOpen the room link in Chrome on the computer at the projector. Start talks with a button, your clicker works, partner loop runs during breaks.

What it does

  • The right screensSlides on the projector, notes on the speaker monitor. Just a laptop and a projector? Controls and notes share one window.
  • Phone as monitor and clickerThe speaker scans a QR code: notes, time and slides on the phone, big buttons to move on. A Help button calls the technician.
  • Videos and soundPowerPoint videos play right on the slide, even offline. Sound goes to the projector over HDMI or to the audio interface you pick.
  • Works offlineEverything is downloaded in advance. If the wifi dies, the talk goes on.
  • Preview for organizersEvery slide next to the speaker's notes. You know what's coming before the audience does.
  • Partner loopSponsor photos and videos during breaks and between talks, with a "Next: …" bar.
  • Door signA TV by the room shows "Now / Next" from what's really happening inside. Use your own background.
  • Programme from ExcelPaste the table from Excel or Google Sheets and the programme is done. Running late? Shift it with one click.
  • Questions and pollsA QR code on the screen, the audience asks and votes from their phones, results live on the big screen. No Slido, no sign-up.
  • Panel discussionsA slide with the topic, moderator and panelists, or your own background. Audience Q&A works here too.
  • Works with AIDictate the programme to your AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT or any other with MCP) and ask it who still owes you slides. MCP server and REST API included.
  • Cleans up after itselfDownload everything in one ZIP, then the event deletes itself. No data left behind.

FAQ

What is FckScrn?

An open-source tool for conferences, meetups and company events. The organizer puts the programme together, speakers upload their slides via their own link and the room computer downloads everything in advance. Each talk then starts in one click on the right screens: slides on the projector, notes and a timer on the speaker monitor.

Do I need to install anything on the room computer?

No. Open the room link in Chrome or Edge and allow access to screens. The projection goes fullscreen with a single key press, then it runs by itself.

What if the internet goes down?

Nothing happens. Presentations and the partner loop are downloaded to the browser in advance and play from there.

Does it work with PowerPoint and Keynote?

Yes. Speakers upload PowerPoint, Keynote, PDF or OpenOffice. The browser plays a PDF version; the desktop app can also run the original, e.g. for animations.

Do I need Slido for audience questions?

No. Every talk and panel has its own QR code in the corner of the projection. The audience asks from their phones without signing up and votes for the questions they care about. The speaker isn't distracted by them mid-talk; the technician puts them on the big screen with one button.

How much does it cost?

The open-source version is free on your own computer or server. The online version costs €59 per event with one room and €199 per event with up to five rooms. You pay once per event, no subscription.

What happens to the data?

The event deletes itself, files included, 14 days after it takes place. Before that you can download everything in one ZIP, or delete it right away.

Why open source?

Because presentations at events should just work. Anyone can run FckScrn for free. If you'd rather not deal with servers, use the online version.
