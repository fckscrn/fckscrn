2. What the service is

The service helps show presentations at events: the organizer builds the programme, speakers upload their slides through their own links, and the computer in the room downloads everything in advance and plays it on the right screens. It also includes audience questions and polls, a partner loop, the speaker's phone as a remote and an event page.

No sign-up is needed. Access to an event's admin is a secret link the organizer receives when creating the event. Whoever has the link can manage the event; the organizer keeps it like a password and creates a new one in the admin if it leaks.

3. Trial mode

Every new event is free in trial mode. The whole service works; the projection just shows a “Zkušební režim” (trial) watermark. Trial mode is for trying the service and gives no further rights.

4. Ordering and the contract

  • The organizer orders an event in its admin with Activate event: enters billing details, agrees to these terms and sends the order.
  • Sending the order concludes a contract for the service for that event. The event is activated right away and the watermark disappears.
  • The organizer is responsible for correct billing details.

5. Price and payment

  • The price is per event according to the price list valid at the time of the order: one room CZK 1,490, several rooms (up to 5) CZK 4,990. The provider is not a VAT payer; the price is final.
  • After the order, the event admin shows a proforma invoice under Invoices, due in 14 days (bank transfer, QR payment). A proforma is a request for payment, not a tax document.
  • Once the payment arrives, the provider issues the invoice, available in the same section, usually by the next business day.
  • If the proforma is not paid within 7 days after its due date and after a reminder, the provider may return the event to trial mode (with the watermark). Paying activates it again.
  • Payments are matched by the variable symbol (reference). Without it or with a different amount the payment may not be matched automatically; an e-mail helps.

  • Audience questions are visible to everyone with the questions link. The organizer can hide them.

7. Storage and deletion
